About BetterWorldNow

The logo's "B" is built from two steps, not a straight line — because that's how this project actually grew.

Step One

A small research project

BetterWorldNow began as a Design of Interactive Systems project at IIIT Delhi. A small team ran a 116-response survey and 11 field interviews around Nehru Place and on campus, trying to understand one question: why don't more people act on environmental issues they say they care about? The answer that kept coming back wasn't apathy — it was a lack of motivation and incentive, a hypothesis the team confirmed with a chi-squared test on the data. That single insight became the whole idea: pair learning with real, visible rewards instead of building another awareness-only site.

Step Two

Taken further, for real impact

What started as a class project didn't stop there. Pratham and Shreya picked it up and kept building — turning the original research into the actual product you're using: the points system, the leaderboard, the quizzes and facts, the rewards. The goal shifted from "prove the idea works" to "make it something people actually come back to," and that's the version that's live today.

Why the Steps

Growth, one step at a time

The two stepped blocks in the logo aren't just a shape — they're the point. Nobody goes from doing nothing to living zero-waste overnight, and no project goes from a class assignment to a real product in one leap either. Both happen the same way: a small step, held onto, followed by another one. Green, because that's the destination this is all in service of.
